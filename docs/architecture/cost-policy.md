# Cost policy

The required SyncWatch workflows must operate without additional subscription, API, hosting or domain fees. Basic use must work with ordinary free Google accounts. An organizer's paid Google Workspace subscription, temporary trial or promotional credit does not satisfy this requirement.

Use existing devices for self-hosting and services with a permanent free allowance. Do not enable billing, payment methods, paid resources, billable overages or paid quota increases. Exhausting a free allowance must stop the affected requests with a clear error while independent local playback remains available.

Check current provider terms and account eligibility separately. A free API can still restrict an operation to particular account types. Successful OAuth consent, an enabled API and a valid access token do not establish that a free account can create a meeting.

Google currently describes standard Meet REST API use as available at no additional cost and warns of planned charges for over-quota requests. Keep within free limits and do not enable billing to obtain additional capacity. See [Google Meet usage limits and pricing](https://developers.google.com/workspace/meet/api/guides/limits#pricing).

Preserve the selected provider, authorization boundaries and all-three desktop requirements. If a required workflow cannot be delivered under free terms, keep it incomplete and obtain an explicit product decision between concrete free alternatives. Do not silently replace the workflow, broaden account access or introduce a paid dependency.
